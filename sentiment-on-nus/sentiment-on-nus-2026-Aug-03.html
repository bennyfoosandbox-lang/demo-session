2. Incoming Chief Justice Sushil Nair, an NUS Law alumnus, uses his first public speech to pledge a crackdown on bullying in the legal profession

3. NUS Law alumni mark the faculty's homecoming to Kent Ridge after nearly two decades at Bukit Timah

On 25 July, alumni spanning the inaugural Class of 1961 through the Class of 2026 gathered at NUS Law's new Kent Ridge premises to reconnect and unveil a commemorative artwork marking the move. 4. NUS economists argue in the Straits Times that Singapore's electric-bus rollout shows carbon accounting is "only half the story"

In a 30 July commentary, Assoc Profs Alberto Salvo and Timothy Wong (NUS Economics) and Zhihao Han (Asia Competitiveness Institute, NUS Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy) argue that public appreciation for electric buses rests as much on quieter, smoother rides and cleaner air as on abstract carbon-reduction metrics, and that policy communication should lean more on those tangible, felt benefits. It's the latest in a steady run of NUS faculty op-eds landing in Singapore's mainstream press that this digest has tracked through July.

Sources: NUS Newsroom (syndicating Straits Times op-ed)
